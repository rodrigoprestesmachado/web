How Microservices Work

Microservices architecture structures an application as a collection of small, independent services that communicate over well-defined APIs.

7. Benefits of Microservices

Scalability

Scale individual services based on demand, not the entire application.

Independent Development

Teams can work on different services simultaneously with different technologies.

Resilience

Failure in one service doesn't necessarily bring down the entire system.

Continuous Deployment

Easier to update and deploy individual services without full redeployment.
