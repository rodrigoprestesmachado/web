# Rodrigo Prestes Machado

[![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Discord%20-%20Rodrigo%20Prestes%20Machado%20-%20%237792C8)](https://discord.gg/C29cqvm)

Rodrigo Prestes Machado holds a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the
Catholic University of Pelotas (UCPel, 2001), a Master's degree in Computer
Science from the Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio,
2005), and a PhD in Computer Science and Education from the Federal University
of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS, 2019). Since 2010, he has been a professor at the
Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology of Rio Grande do Sul
(IFRS), Porto Alegre Campus. In 2024, he also joined the Universidad Carlos III
de Madrid (UC3M), Spain, as an Honorary Professor. His current research interest
lies in the development of systems based on generative artificial intelligence
to enhance learning experiences and improve academic outcomes.
